I also included the standard formatting requirements.  
 

The Vandalizer RFA output should automatically include the standard required proposal components identified in NSF_NIH_USDA_NIFA_DOE_NASA.pdf, even when the funding announcement does not explicitly list all required submission elements.

If the RFP, RFA, NOFO or other funding solicitation provides specific formatting or document preparation instructions, those instructions should be used in the Vandalizer RFA output. If the solicitation does not provide specific guidance, the Vandalizer RFA output should populate formatting requirements from the sponsor-specific standards contained in the NSF_NIH_USDA_NIFA_DOE_NASA.pdf reference document.
